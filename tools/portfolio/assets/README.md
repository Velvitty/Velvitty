# Photographic demo assets

Two original image assets generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-05. They are illustrative samples, not source-application processing results. Both are encoded as WebP at the original 1536 × 1024 dimensions and embedded once in the standalone HTML. No external asset host or runtime download is used.

- `coast.webp`: photographic texture and tone example for Posity and Film Filter Studio; also used in the hero prints and project gallery.
- `botanical.webp`: fine detail and composition example for Upscaly and Photo Scorer; also used in the hero prints and project gallery.

## Generation prompts

**Coast**

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: large photographic sample for an elegant interactive photography portfolio, not a UI mockup. Create a cinematic editorial landscape photograph, wide 3:2 composition: sculptural pale limestone sea cliffs on the left, a tiny coastal path, luminous calm blue sea, gentle ripples, an open horizon toward the upper third, distant layered headlands fading into haze. Late afternoon soft sunlight, pearl white, delicate shell pink reflections and airy sky blue, restrained natural saturation, very fine detail and true photographic depth, generous ocean space. Visually arresting, expensive fine art travel photography. No people close up, no text, no logos, no border, no watermark. Rich real detail for before/after film and sharpness comparison demos.

Create a wide 3:2 editorial macro photograph of translucent pale pink cosmos petals and creamy white anemone blossoms with tiny dewdrops, a single sharply focused flower slightly right of center, slender elegant stems crossing through foreground, distant petals creating layered soft bokeh. Early morning light, subtle lemon sunlight, dusty pink and cool grey blue shadows, delicate tonal gradients, realistic detail, clean natural color, strong optical depth. Quietly spectacular fine art photography with an excellent focal point and composition. No text, no logos, no watermark, no border, not an illustration.
